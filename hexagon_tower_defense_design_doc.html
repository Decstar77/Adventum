Hexform — Game Design Document

HEXFORM
// design document v0.1 — top-down hex colony defense
Concept

A real-time top-down survival builder. Players chain hexagonal tiles into a growing colony — farms, generators, walls, turrets — while enemy waves continuously pressure the base. Large surge events hit at intervals, demanding reactive thinking and smart expansion.

Design pillars
Chain reaction
Every tile placed unlocks new placements. Building is tactile and spatial.
Constant pressure
Enemies never stop. Surges punish poor positioning hard.
Readable chaos
Simple shapes. Fast reads. No information overload.
Hex tile types
CORE
Your base. Game ends if destroyed.
FARM
Generates food over time.
GENERATOR
Produces energy. Powers adjacent tiles.
TURRET
Attacks enemies in range. Basic damage.
WALL
High HP. Slows enemy pathing.
RELAY
Extends build range from core.
Wave structure

Enemies spawn continuously from map edges. Pressure slowly ramps, punctuated by surge events every ~90 seconds that send coordinated large groups.

Enemy types
Crawler
Low HP, fast. Floods in large groups. Main surge filler.
Brute
High HP, slow. Tanks turret fire. Targets walls.
Piercer
Bypasses walls. Medium HP. Seeks Core directly.
Swarmhost
Spawns crawlers when killed. Kill last in groups.
Resource economy
Food Generated by farms. Required to place most tiles.
Energy Produced by generators. Powers turrets and relays.
Scrap Dropped by enemies. Used to upgrade existing tiles.

Player places hex tiles adjacent to existing ones, radiating outward from the Core. Turrets need energy. Relays let you build further out. Farms fund rapid expansion. The colony grows until enemies overwhelm it — there is no hard win condition in the base mode.

Scope notes

v1 targetMVP: Core, Farm, Generator, Turret, Wall, Relay tiles. Crawlers + Brutes. Surge system. Single flat map.

Post-launchlater: Piercer, Swarmhost enemies. Tile upgrades via scrap. Multiple biome maps. Tile combos / adjacency bonuses (e.g. turret next to generator = +fire rate). Conditional surge variants (night surges, armored waves).
