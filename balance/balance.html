How the simulation works

The "buildout" model assumes the player follows the chosen strategy: starting at t = 0 they spend whatever food is on hand on the strategy's queue (e.g. 5 farms first, then turrets/walls). Food income at each instant equals (farms · rate). Surges are scheduled per the wave timing fields; each surge's required DPS = (incoming HP) / surge_duration. The "turrets needed" line divides surge DPS by the assumed turret tier mix in the strategy. This is a rough analytic model — it does not simulate aim cones, pathing, or HP attrition mid-surge.
